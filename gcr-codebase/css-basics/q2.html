Jaipur - The Pink City

HistoryPlacesCulture

History

Jaipur is the capital of Rajasthan and is famous for its planned streets, forts and royal heritage.

Famous Places

Visitors can explore Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal and Jantar Mantar.

Culture

Jaipur is known for colourful textiles, handicrafts, traditional music and delicious vegetarian food.
