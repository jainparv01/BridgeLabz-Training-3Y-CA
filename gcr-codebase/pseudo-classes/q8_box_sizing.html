Box-Sizing Comparison

Default: content-box

Width: 300px
Padding: 30px
Border: 5px

Total outer width becomes 370px.

border-box

Width: 300px
Padding: 30px
Border: 5px

Total outer width stays 300px.

With content-box, padding and borders are added to the declared width. With border-box, they are included inside the declared width.
